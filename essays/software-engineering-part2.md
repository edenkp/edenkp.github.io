## Continuing Down the Path

The course, ICS 414, is the continuation of ICS 314. In this class, students like myself take our software engineering skills to another level by using those skills to create an application for an actual client. I was assigned to a group that originally started out with 8 people, but then became a group of 6 down the line. Taking what we had learned from this class, we worked together and collaborated to create our version of a financial model that serves almost the same purpose as an Excecl sheet, but as a website application. 

## The Start of our Ascent

Starting at the very beginning, we needed to make up a group name to represent our group when we were to present our project during the different milestones throughout the semester. Originally inspired by someone who mistakingly joined our group in the first few days, we called ourselves "Pineapple Spire". Then came the part where we needed to create our vision: our project. For this part, my groupmates and I focused heavily on setting up the front end of our application, such as adding our logo, buttons, different pages, etc. In terms of data, all of that was hardcoded so we could at least show what we were envisioning for our project. For me, one of my main tasks was to set up Stress Test 5. Using the skills I had learned from ICS 314, I set up what I had envisioned for the Stress Test 5 page. I set up the different tables and input boxes. Although these input boxes weren't connected to a database or fully working, as well as having hardcoded data, it was there for the sake of showing my vision for that specific page.
